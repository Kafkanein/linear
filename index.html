03 / THE UNUSUAL PART

This *is* the application.

Rather than claim that I know how to talk about AI, here's a working artifact that makes specific choices under constraints.

01

Actual learned model.

Regression parameters are fitted in your browser to synthetic delivery history. Holdout error is computed, not hard-coded.

02

Optimization with teeth.

A tiny exhaustive assignment search balances predicted delays and distance, while hard policy rules can reject tempting but unsafe options.

03

No fake agent.

This demo uses an interpretable model plus explicit decisions, not an LLM behind a decorative chat box. Agents would come after identity, tools, permissions and evaluation.
